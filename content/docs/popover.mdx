## API

**Main exports:** `Popover`, `PopoverTrigger`, `PopoverClose`, `PopoverContent`,
`PopoverHeader`, `PopoverTitle`, `PopoverDescription`, `PopoverPortal`,
`PopoverPositioner`, `PopoverPopup`, and `createPopoverHandle`.

- `Popover` supports `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `onOpenChangeComplete`,
  `actionsRef`, `modal`, `handle`, and trigger payloads through Base UI. Open-change
  details report the reason and allow cancellation.
- `PopoverTrigger` accepts `disabled`, `openOnHover`, `delay`, `closeDelay`, `handle`,
  and `payload`. Its `render` prop composes a Button or another compatible element.
- `PopoverContent` combines a portal, positioner, and popup. `side="bottom"`,
  `align="center"`, and `sideOffset={6}` set its preferred placement.
- `positionerProps` forwards collision boundaries, collision avoidance, padding
  (8px by default), alignment offsets, a custom anchor, sticky positioning, positioning
  method, refs, and state-based classes.
- `portalProps` forwards `container`, `keepMounted`, refs, and other portal props.
- `autoSize` defaults to `true`: the panel animates to its content height. Set it to
  `false` when providing a fixed height or your own sizing behavior.
- `contentClassName` styles the content wrapper inside the scroll viewport. Use it for
  content layout, spacing, and gaps; `className` styles the panel itself.
- `PopoverContent` and `PopoverPopup` accept `initialFocus`, `finalFocus`, `render`,
  DOM props, and refs. Their `className` may be a string or a function receiving open,
  side, alignment, transition, and instant state. The positioner, title, and description
  also preserve their respective Base UI state-based class callbacks.
- `PopoverHeader` accepts ordinary div props and a ref. Title and description automatically
  label and describe the panel. `PopoverClose` composes the caller's close control.

## Resizing and overflow

The panel follows content as it loads, expands, or collapses. Continuous size changes,
such as an expanding textarea or an already-animated calendar, follow directly rather
than accumulating animation lag. Height is intentionally animated because the surface
must grow around its content. The panel opens and closes with the Select family's motion. Durations use the library's motion tokens and collapse
under reduced motion.

Available screen space limits the panel's height and width. Tall content scrolls through
the shared Scroll Area component, with its scrollbar and edge fades. Scrollbars stay hidden during
a height resize and return only if content still overflows after it settles. Titles, descriptions,
and ordinary content wrap long words and URLs. The panel keeps its existing padding;
`className="p-0"` still works for content that supplies its own, such as Calendar.

## Shared triggers

`createPopoverHandle<Payload>()` connects triggers anywhere in the React tree to a single
`Popover`. Keep the handle stable. Each trigger supplies a typed `payload`; the root's
function child receives the active trigger's payload. Closing returns focus to the active
trigger. `PopoverProps`, `PopoverTriggerProps`, and `PopoverHandle` preserve the payload type.

## Portals and composition

Use `portalProps.container` to choose a mounting container and `portalProps.keepMounted`
to preserve child state while closed. Kept-mounted content remains hidden and outside
keyboard navigation. The default unmounts after the closing transition.

For a custom structure, compose `PopoverPortal`, `PopoverPositioner`, and `PopoverPopup`
separately. `PopoverPopup` retains automatic sizing, Scroll Area, and motion. Do not wrap
`PopoverContent` in another portal or positioner; it already includes both.

## Focus and dismissal

The default is non-modal: the surrounding page remains interactive. `modal={true}` blocks
outside interactions and applies Base UI's modal scrolling behavior; include a
`PopoverClose` inside to enable focus trapping and give touch screen readers a way out.
`modal="trap-focus"` traps focus without locking page scrolling.

Escape and outside interaction close the panel. Nested popovers and controls such as Select
use Base UI's shared dismissal behavior, so interaction inside a child keeps its parent open
and Escape closes the topmost layer. Hover opening, controlled state, custom focus targets,
and collision positioning are available independently of the demo settings.
