## API

**Main export:** `Slider`

A controlled or uncontrolled numeric slider with a spring-gliding fill and three thumb variants:
`bar` for a thin handle, `circle` for a round thumb centered in a thick rounded rail whose fill
encapsulates it and remains behind it at the minimum, and `knob` for a round thumb over a thin
rail. The knob rests one size step smaller and grows to its former resting size while dragged.
Optional display props include `label`, `showValue`, and `showTicks`. Set `range` to render two
independently draggable thumbs and a fill between them; pass `[start, end]` to `value` or
`defaultValue`, and the range callback receives the updated tuple. Without `range`, the component
keeps its single numeric value API.
`inlineLabel` moves the label and the value inside the bar, one at each end, in place of the row
above it. The thumb dims as it passes under either, in proportion to how far it has crossed, so the text
stays readable and the fade reverses as the thumb leaves. It dims to an opaque colour rather
than going translucent, so the rail's ticks never show through the handle. It applies to the `bar` thumb
only and is ignored by `circle` and `knob`, whose rails are too thin to hold a line of text.

`showValueTooltip` is off by default; enable it to show the live value above the thumb while the
thumb is hovered or keyboard-focused. It stays visible during pointer drag, follows the thumb
continuously during motion, and closes after release when the pointer is no longer over the thumb.
