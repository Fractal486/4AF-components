## API

**Main export:** `Slider`

A controlled or uncontrolled numeric slider with a spring-gliding fill and three thumb variants:
`bar` for a thin handle, `circle` for a round thumb centered in a thick rounded rail whose fill
encapsulates it and remains behind it at the minimum, and `knob` for a small round dot on a thin
rail (4px for `md`, 2px for `sm`) it overhangs entirely. The knob grows by a quarter while dragged. Optional display
props include `label`, `showValue`, and `showTicks`; the `knob` rail carries the fill alone and
renders no ticks to keep the thin rail clear. Set `range` to render two
independently draggable thumbs and a fill between them; pass `[start, end]` to `value` or
`defaultValue`, and the range callback receives the updated tuple. Without `range`, the component
keeps its single numeric value API.
`inlineLabel` moves the label and the value inside the bar, one at each end, in place of the row
above it. Within 8px of either, the thumb dodges: it fades to 15% opacity and drops the grab stretch,
returning to its resting height, and restores as it leaves. At either end of the rail it never dodges.
The thumb carries `data-dodged` while dodging. Under reduced motion only the fade remains. The inline value is `muted-foreground` at rest and `foreground` while the slider
is in use. It applies to the `bar` thumb only and is ignored by `circle` and `knob`, whose rails are
too thin to hold a line of text.

On the `bar` thumb, ticks are hidden at rest and the fill is `foreground/15`. While the slider is in
use (hovered, dragged, or keyboard-focused) the ticks fade in and the fill steps up to
`foreground/20`. `circle` and `knob` keep their ticks and fill constant.

`showValueTooltip` is off by default; enable it to show the live value above the thumb while the
thumb is hovered or keyboard-focused. It stays visible during pointer drag, follows the thumb
continuously during motion, and closes after release when the pointer is no longer over the thumb.
