## API

**Main export:** `Slider`

| Prop | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `orientation` | `"horizontal"` | `"horizontal"` or `"vertical"`. Give a vertical slider or its parent an explicit height. |
| `draggableRange` | `false` | Drag between two handles to move the selected interval while preserving its width. |
| `animateValue` | `false` | Opt in to rolling digits and blur for the displayed values and thumb tooltips. |
| `format` | — | `Intl.NumberFormatOptions` for the displayed values and tooltips, including currency, percent, and units. |
| `locale` | — | Locale used for number formatting. |
| `getAriaValueText` | — | `(formattedValue, value, index) => string` supplies a spoken value for each handle. |
| `getAriaLabel` | — | `(index) => string` supplies an accessible name for each handle. |
| `className` | — | A string or `(state) => string`, including orientation, values, disabled, and dragging state. |
| `render` | `<div>` | A replacement element or `(props, state) => element`; forward the supplied props and ref. |
| `onValueChange` | — | `(value, details) => void`. Receives a number or range tuple; `details.cancel()` rejects a change. |
| `onValueCommitted` | — | Runs when a pointer interaction finishes or a keyboard change commits. |

A controlled or uncontrolled numeric slider. Defaults are `thumb="bar"`, `size="md"`,
`min={0}`, `max={100}`, and `step={1}`. `showValue` and `showTicks` default to `true`;
`showValueTooltip` defaults to `false`. Ticks are hidden when the range has more than 50 steps
and are never shown for `thumb="knob"`.
Fractional steps keep every eligible tick, including the final tick when `max` lies on the
step grid. The bar thumb intentionally omits the rail's end marks.

A dragged thumb tracks the pointer 1:1. With fewer than 50 steps across the range, it slides
from step to step instead, from 0.05s for small steps up to 0.25s for steps half the range
wide. Track clicks, keyboard steps, and values set from outside
glide on a softer spring. Under reduced motion every change is instant.

Set `--slider-fill` to change the fill colour; it defaults to the foreground at 15%. While the bar
is in use, the fill mixes in 8% foreground.

```tsx
<Slider aria-label="Volume" style={{ "--slider-fill": "var(--color-ring)" } as React.CSSProperties} />
```

Value readouts and thumb tooltips update immediately by default. Set `animateValue` to use
[Number Animation](/docs/number-animation) for rolling digits with motion blur. They keep the root's `locale` and `format`, and reduced motion shows updates immediately.
The editor remains a normal input while typing.

With `showValue`, the number is editable: it shows a text cursor on hover,
and a click turns it into an input. Enter or leaving the field commits the typed value, clamped
to the range and snapped to the step; Escape cancels. A press on the number never starts a drag.
A range shows one editable number per thumb, and a typed value stops at the other thumb.
Snapping starts at `min` and preserves its decimal precision as well as the step's, including
steps such as `1e-7`. For example, `min={0.5}` and `step={1}` snap a typed `3` to `3.5`.
After Escape, clicking the value starts a fresh edit that can be committed normally.

| `thumb` | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| `bar` | A fader in a hairline-bordered rail. The fill's straight edge marks the value across the full width, empty at the minimum. A thin 2px thumb rides 8px inside its edge, parked 8px from either end, and grows taller and slightly wider while grabbed. Ticks sit at the value positions, without the end marks, in ruler heights: tallest at the midpoint and taller every fifth step. The tick at the fill's edge is hidden. In range mode the fill reaches 8px past each thumb. |
| `circle` | A round thumb inside a thick rounded rail. The fill wraps the thumb and stays behind it at the minimum. |
| `knob` | A small dot on a thin rail (4px at `md`, 2px at `sm`) that it overhangs. It grows by 25% at `md` and one third at `sm` while dragged. Its rail shows the fill only, never ticks. |

`range` renders two thumbs with a fill between them. Passing `[start, end]` to `value` or
`defaultValue` also enables range mode, even without `range`. The range callback receives the
updated tuple; single-thumb mode uses a number.

With `draggableRange`, dragging between the handles moves both together using `step` and stops
at `min` or `max` without narrowing the interval. Mouse, pen, and touch are supported; each
handle remains independently draggable and keyboard-operable. A click without dragging still
moves the nearest handle. Whole-range changes have `details.reason="drag"` and
`details.activeThumbIndex=-1`; the root carries `data-range-dragging` and reports
`state.dragging=true` to `className` and `render`. Releasing a changed range fires
`onValueCommitted` once. A canceled pointer gesture does not commit.

Inside the selected interval, drag the visible handle to move one endpoint; its invisible
hit padding does not intercept whole-range dragging.
Range gestures respect disabled state inherited from Base UI's `Field.Root`, including when
the field becomes disabled during a gesture; a disabled gesture does not commit a value.

Vertical sliders run from the minimum at the bottom to the maximum at the top. All three
thumb styles and both sizes work vertically; labels and values stay upright above the rail.
Horizontal sliders follow the surrounding reading direction.

The visible `label` names the slider input, including with `inlineLabel`. Without a visible
label, supply `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; an explicit accessible label takes precedence
over `label`. Range handles append “minimum” and “maximum” to the shared name. Set
`thumbLabels={["Lowest price", "Highest price"]}` to give the two handles custom names instead.

## Bar thumb

On the `bar` thumb, ticks are hidden at rest and the fill is `foreground/15`. While the slider is
in use (hovered, dragged, or keyboard-focused), eligible ticks fade in and the fill mixes in
8% additional foreground. `circle` and `knob` keep the fill constant; eligible circle ticks
stay visible.

`inlineLabel` moves the label and the value inside the bar, one at each end, replacing the row
above it. Within 8px of either, the thumb dodges: it fades to 15% opacity and drops its grab
stretch, then restores as it moves away. It never dodges at either end of the rail. The thumb
carries `data-dodged` while dodging; under reduced motion only the fade remains. The inline value
is `muted-foreground` at rest and `foreground` while the slider is in use. `circle` and `knob`
ignore `inlineLabel`, as do vertical sliders.

## Value tooltip

`showValueTooltip` (off by default) shows the live value above the thumb while it is hovered or
keyboard-focused. It stays open while dragging, follows the thumb, and closes after release once
the pointer has left the thumb. Vertical sliders place the tooltip beside the thumb. Both
tooltips stay visible during a whole-range drag. Tooltips use the same `format` and `locale`
as the displayed values; editing still accepts a plain number.

`getAriaValueText` changes what assistive technology reads without changing visible formatting.
It receives the formatted text, raw number, and handle index. `getAriaLabel` can name handles
by index; `thumbLabels` remains available for a fixed pair of range names.

In Windows high-contrast mode, rails receive a visible outline, the selected interval uses
the system highlight colour, and handles and keyboard focus use system colours.

The bar thumb exposes `data-grabbed` while stretched by a drag, and `data-dodged` when it
moves out of the way of an inline label. Reduced motion skips the grab stretch.
