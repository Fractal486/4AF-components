## API

**Main exports:** `Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab`, `TabsCount`, `TabsPanel`

Base UI tabs with a travelling indicator; `segment`, `pill`, and `underline` variants; an
`appearance` for segment and pill; and two sizes.

| Part | Props and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `Tabs` | `value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`; `orientation="horizontal" \| "vertical"` (default `horizontal`); `variant`, `appearance`, and `size`. |
| `TabsList` | Automatically scrolls along its orientation when constrained. `maxWidth` optionally adds a width limit; numbers are pixels. `activateOnFocus` defaults to `false`; `loopFocus` defaults to `true`. |
| `TabsTab` | `value`, `disabled`, and arbitrary children, including icons and counts. |
| `TabsPanel` | `value` and `keepMounted` (default `true`). Inactive panels retain their content and state unless `keepMounted={false}` is supplied. |
| `TabsCount` | A dim, tabular count after the label. Accepts native span attributes and refs. |

`Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab`, and `TabsPanel` forward refs and native props and accept Base UI's `render`
prop, either a replacement element or a render function. Replacing a panel keeps its tab-panel semantics,
event handlers, refs, and content entrance animation. Preserve the supplied props when using a render
function.

Their `className` and `style` also accept state functions. Root/list state includes `orientation`; tab
state includes `active` and `disabled`; panel state includes `hidden`. String classes work as before.

| `appearance` | Track and selected chip |
| --- | --- |
| `default` | Muted track; raised `surface` chip with a hairline. |
| `solid` | `border` track with a borderless `background` chip. |
| `outline` | Bordered, unfilled track; `bg-muted` fill under the selected tab. Pill height matches `default`; segment tracks are 2px taller. |

`underline` ignores `appearance`. The `solid` boolean is deprecated: `solid` maps to
`appearance="solid"`, and an explicit `appearance` wins over it.

| Variant | Outlined geometry |
| --- | --- |
| `segment` | Fill inset 3px from the border. 34px (`md`) / 30px (`sm`) tracks, the Input and Select heights; 8px corners around 4px fills (`md`), 7px around 3px (`sm`). |
| `pill` | Same padding as the filled pill; the tabs give up 1px of vertical padding to the border. Fully rounded. |

`TabsCount` renders a dim, tabular count after a tab's label, in any variant:

```tsx
<TabsTab value="included">
  Included <TabsCount>3</TabsCount>
</TabsTab>
```

`size` is `md` by default. `sm` uses a smaller type step and tighter padding, with a 32px pill
track. Segment corners step down with it, from an 8px track around 6px tabs to a 7px track around
5px tabs. Pill tabs are fully rounded, and underline tabs round only their top corners. The horizontal underline indicator spans the selected tab's label plus a small overhang (4px at `md`, 2px at `sm`), not its full padding.

## Scrolling and edge fades

All variants scroll automatically when their tabs exceed the available space. Give the parent a definite
width for horizontal lists. `maxWidth` is an optional additional limit: use a number for pixels
(`maxWidth={280}`) or a CSS length (`maxWidth="20rem"`). The list never grows past its parent's width.

With `orientation="vertical"` on `Tabs`, tabs stack beside the panels and the indicator moves vertically.
The underline becomes a side marker. Constrain the list's height with `className` or `style` when needed;
overflowing vertical lists scroll with fades at the top and bottom. Reading direction also moves the
side marker to the appropriate edge.

An edge fades with a tapered blur while more tabs are hidden on that side, including the track and the
underline variant's rule. Touch and trackpad scrolling work natively, with the scrollbar hidden.
Clicking a tab in either half of the list scrolls it toward the centre when more content is hidden on
that side. Keyboard focus, the initial selection, and controlled selection changes also bring hidden
tabs into view. Under reduced motion the scrolling is instant. Focus rings sit inside the tabs so they
aren't clipped.

## Icons

Place an SVG inside `TabsTab` alongside its label. Icons get consistent spacing, do not shrink, and
default to 14px at `sm` or 16px at `md`. An explicit `size-*` class on an icon overrides that default.
Give icon-only tabs an accessible label, and mark decorative icons `aria-hidden`.

## Motion and initial rendering

Base UI positions the indicator before React hydration, so server-rendered tabs can show their active
selection immediately. Selection changes animate its position and dimensions; resizing snaps it into
place. Dimensions animate to preserve the chip's rounded corners without distorting them.
Indicator timing follows `--duration-slower`, `--duration-slow`, and `--ease-out`; reduced motion removes
the transition. Content panels keep their existing short fade and upward entrance, with no motion under
reduced motion.
